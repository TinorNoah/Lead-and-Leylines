# Farming

Pots and area harvest. Seasons are in [Seasons](seasons.md). HarvestCraft crops are in [Food](food.md). Oh The Trees You'll Grow is listed with Oh The Biomes We've Gone in [Biomes](biomes.md).

Each mod is listed once. Decision notes stay in [`docs/mods/`](../mods/manifest.md).
