# Magic

Spell mods and their addons. Gun affixes for TaCZ and Point Blank are in [Weapons](weapons.md). Cooking spells are listed here because they extend Iron's Spells.

Each mod is listed once. Decision notes stay in [`docs/mods/`](../mods/manifest.md).
