# Weapons

Guns, gun packs, and the addons that change those guns. Melee movesets and parkour are in [Combat](combat.md). Apotheosis is in [Magic](magic.md).

Each mod is listed once. Decision notes stay in [`docs/mods/`](../mods/manifest.md).
